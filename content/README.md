# Curriculum source material

The `paragraphs` fields for Chapters 1–3 reproduce Alex Julian’s _AI Yoga Masters_ (2024), published here at the author’s request. Copyright © 2024 Alex Julian Yoga. All rights reserved. These source passages are excluded from the repository’s MIT license. Lesson titles and numbering follow the original curriculum.

Print page footers and PDF layout whitespace have been removed. Wording is preserved; the linked PDF is the authoritative source. The `practice`, `reflection`, and `note` fields are new AI-assisted editorial adaptations, separate from the original text. They do not represent a completed scientific or historical evidence review.


Chapter 2 (Lessons 10–17) is extracted from PDF pages 35–48 (printed pages 30–43). Paragraphs split across pages are rejoined; line wraps, page footers, and lesson headings are removed from the paragraph fields. Original wording, including typographical errors, is retained.

Chapter 3 (Lessons 18–30) is extracted from PDF pages 50–70 (printed pages 45–65), using the same whitespace and page-continuation rules. All thirteen lessons were checked against the extracted source after whitespace normalization.
